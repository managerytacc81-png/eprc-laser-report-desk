## Part 3: Quality Control & Radiation Safety Testing Summary (21 CFR 1002.30)

1. **Production Quality Testing Volume:**
   - **Total Units Tested:** 7,400 units (100% production line optical power testing)
   - **Non-Conforming Units Detected:** 11 units (0.15% defect rate)
   - **Disposition of Rejected Units:** All non-conforming units quarantined, repaired, and re-tested prior to final packing.

2. **Corrective Actions Taken:**
   - Identified loose interlock microswitch bracket on 11 units during mechanical burn-in. Tooling tightened and torque specs updated in standard work instruction SWI-ENG-042.

3. **Measurement System Calibration:**
   - Test equipment NIST-traceable: Yes
   - Standard: Thorlabs Optical Calibration Center, NIST Traceable Standard Cert #77812
